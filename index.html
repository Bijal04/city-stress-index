How it works

Five dimensions of urban stress

Each city is scored daily across five key urban health factors, combining real-time data from multiple global sources.

🚦
Traffic
Congestion levels, commute times, and road network efficiency scored via TomTom Traffic API.
💨
Air quality
PM2.5, NO2, ozone and AQI measurements sourced from OpenAQ global sensor network.
🌤
Weather
Temperature extremes, humidity, and severe weather alerts via OpenWeather and Open-Meteo.
🔒
Safety
Crime indices, incident reports, and public safety scores from Numbeo's global database.
💰
Cost of living
Housing, groceries, transport, and overall affordability from Numbeo's cost-of-living index.
